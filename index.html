Programme Overview

Technology Is Evolving. Governance Must Keep Pace.

In India, no university currently offers an undergraduate degree that specifically trains students to work at the intersection of digital technology, law, policy, and governance.

Digital transformation is creating new challenges around AI regulation, data protection, cybersecurity, platform accountability, and digital public systems.

The B.A. in Digital Governance, Regulation & Policy prepares students to work at the intersection of technology, law, public policy, and international affairs — creating professionals who can help organisations navigate the digital age.
